Back Substitution: an example

We are given an upper triangular matrix UUU from the LULULU decomposition and the vector yyy from the (forward substitution)[forward_example.md] step:

U⋅x=yU\cdot x = yU⋅x=y
(1)

that, in our 4x4 example, becomes:

[β11β12β13β140β22β23β2400β33β34000β44]⋅[x1x2x3x4]=[y1y2y3y4]\begin{bmatrix} \beta_{11} & \beta_{12} & \beta_{13} & \beta_{14} \\ 0 & \beta_{22} & \beta_{23} & \beta_{24} \\ 0 & 0 & \beta_{33} & \beta_{34} \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \beta_{44} \end{bmatrix} \cdot \begin{bmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3 \\ x_4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} y_1 \\ y_2 \\ y_3 \\ y_4 \end{bmatrix}⎣⎡​β11​000​β12​β22​00​β13​β23​β33​0​β14​β24​β34​β44​​⎦⎤​⋅⎣⎡​x1​x2​x3​x4​​⎦⎤​=⎣⎡​y1​y2​y3​y4​​⎦⎤​
(2)

Now we solve for the vector xxx using back substitution.

System to solve:

β11x1+β12x2+β13x3+β14x4=y1β22x2+β23x3+β24x4=y2β33x3+β34x4=y3β44x4=y4\begin{aligned} \beta_{11} x_1 + \beta_{12} x_2 + \beta_{13} x_3 + \beta_{14} x_4 &= y_1 \\ \beta_{22} x_2 + \beta_{23} x_3 + \beta_{24} x_4 &= y_2 \\ \beta_{33} x_3 + \beta_{34} x_4 &= y_3 \\ \beta_{44} x_4 &= y_4 \end{aligned}β11​x1​+β12​x2​+β13​x3​+β14​x4​β22​x2​+β23​x3​+β24​x4​β33​x3​+β34​x4​β44​x4​​=y1​=y2​=y3​=y4​​
(3)

Back Substitution Steps:¶

We will start from the last equation (the bottom row) and work our way upwards, solving for each xix_ixi​ one by one.

1. Solve for x4x_4x4​ from the last equation:¶

x4=y4β44x_4 = \frac{y_4}{\beta_{44}}x4​=β44​y4​​
(4)

2. Solve for x3x_3x3​ from the third equation:¶

x3=1β33(y3−β34x4)x_3 = \frac{1}{\beta_{33}} \left( y_3 - \beta_{34} x_4 \right)x3​=β33​1​(y3​−β34​x4​)
(5)

3. Solve for x2x_2x2​ from the second equation:¶

x2=1β22(y2−β23x3−β24x4)x_2 = \frac{1}{\beta_{22}} \left( y_2 - \beta_{23} x_3 - \beta_{24} x_4 \right)x2​=β22​1​(y2​−β23​x3​−β24​x4​)
(6)

4. Solve for x1x_1x1​ from the first equation:¶

x1=1β11(y1−β12x2−β13x3−β14x4)x_1 = \frac{1}{\beta_{11}} \left( y_1 - \beta_{12} x_2 - \beta_{13} x_3 - \beta_{14} x_4 \right)x1​=β11​1​(y1​−β12​x2​−β13​x3​−β14​x4​)
(7)
